HANZI HUNTER V3.2

V3.2 fixes one-player record timing:
- Saves the completed solo duration as elapsedMs on each one-player record.
- One-player Records now show word count, matching mode and Completed in time.
- Two-player Records continue to show points and individual active time.
- Older V3.1 records without elapsedMs display Not recorded in an earlier version rather than the incorrect 0:00.00.

All V3.1 logo, icons, Help, profile, spinner, cards, timers and recovery features are retained.
