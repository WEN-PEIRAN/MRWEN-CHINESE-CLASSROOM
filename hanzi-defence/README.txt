HANZI DEFENCE  V1.5
1. Copy your vocabulary-data.js into this folder (same place as index.html).
2. Put setup.mp3 and game.mp3 into the sound folder.
3. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge (computer only).

Default controls: E / Enter = launch scout drone, Up/Down = aim, F / Space = fire,
W/S = move turret (after Rail Module), P / Esc = pause. Change keys in Settings.
Firing with no target is allowed, but hitting a Hanzi bug without a target counts as a miss.
Homing Missile gear: one missile, fired automatically on your next shot. It locks onto the bug
closest to where the barrel points (within 15 degrees) and curves around other bugs.
Sound: SOUND button (bottom right) - music and effects on/off + volume. Saved in this browser.
Themed names (planet / sector / mission) are set at the top of app.js (PLANETS, SECTORS, MISSIONS).
